# Yaghan

A microVM-based sandbox platform built on [Firecracker](https://firecracker-microvm.github.io/).

## About the name

Yaghan is named after the **Yaghan** (also known as **Yámana**), the indigenous
people of the southernmost archipelagos of South America — the islands and
channels around Tierra del Fuego, the "Land of Fire." The Yaghan are one of
the southernmost human cultures ever documented, and their descendants are a
small but still-living community, today centred in Chile around Villa
Ukika and Puerto Williams.

The connection is intentional. Firecracker, the kernel-level technology this
project is built on, takes its name from fire; the Land of Fire was named
for the bonfires Yaghan communities kept burning along its shores — visible
to Magellan's crew when they passed in 1520 and the origin of the
region's name. Borrowing the people's name for a project that runs on top
of Firecracker is an acknowledgement of that thread, not a claim on it.
